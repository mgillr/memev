### Fixed

- Op-log recovery keeps every good record: a torn line in the middle of a segment is skipped and reported instead of ending the log.
- Only a torn tail (a partial last line with no newline) is truncated; a complete but unreadable last line is reported and kept.
- append() finishes a short write in a loop and, if it cannot finish, cuts the partial record off again so the next record survives a reopen.
