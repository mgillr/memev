### Added

- The prompt hook now runs recall and the bus pulse in parallel and sends the journal fire-and-forget, so it returns in under 1.5 s even when the backends are slow.

### Fixed

- The prompt hook now ends its process once its output is written, so the app sees the 1.5 s budget: the calls the budget drops and the fire-and-forget journal no longer keep the process open. The journal is accepted as lost when the vault is slow.
- The 1.5 s budget now covers only the prompt hook. The stop hook's contract gate and the pre-compact checkpoint keep their own timeouts, so a slow vault can no longer fail the safety gate open or drop the checkpoint before a compaction.
