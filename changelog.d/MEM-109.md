### Added

- `bus_unread` shows a bounded view: at most eight of the newest unread messages that are not your go, each body cut to 300 characters with a marker when it is longer, and a `not_shown` count for what was left out. A task or an operator hold is shown first, in full and never cut, outside the cap of eight: at most 50 in one call, oldest first, with the rest counted in `not_shown` for the next call. What a capped call does not show stays unread: each later call again shows at most eight, the newest of what is left, and `bus_since` still reaches any message by seq in full.
- A message that arrives later with an older time than what your last call saw (written offline, for example) is shown on the next call, whatever the cap, so it is not buried under newer ones.
- `bus_tail` and `bus_since` show at most 50 messages and report how many more exist (`more`); the hook that fronts new messages to a hooked app reads the whole bus and is not capped.
