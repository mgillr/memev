### Added

- `bus_unread` shows a bounded view: at most eight of the newest unread messages that are not your go, each body cut to 300 characters with a marker when it is longer, and a `not_shown` count for what was left out. A task or an operator hold is always shown first, in full and never cut. What a capped call does not show stays unread, so the next call shows it in its place by time, and `bus_since` still reaches any message by seq in full.
- A message that arrives later with an older time (written offline, for example) is always shown, whatever the cap, so nothing is lost.
- `bus_tail` and `bus_since` show at most 50 messages and report how many more exist (`more`); the hook that fronts new messages to a hooked app reads the whole bus and is not capped.
