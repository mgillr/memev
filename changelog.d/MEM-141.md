## Added

compose.trial.yaml starts the on-prem trial with one command, builds from the repository Dockerfile, keeps the vault in a named volume, publishes the port on loopback only, and declares a health check. The image carries org.opencontainers.image labels for title, description, licence and version. The container entry point runs the doctor checks at start and logs one line per failed or warned check. docs/DOCKER.md documents the three-step trial path and notes that publishing the image to a registry needs the owner go.
