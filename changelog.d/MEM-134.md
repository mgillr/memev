### Added

- A Business trial licence installs only on a business-class identity, into a vault with an organisation; on a personal identity or a personal vault the install is refused with a reason.
- With a trial installed, a join, mount or release from a personal identity, and a mount of a personal vault, are refused, so personal and business stay separate.
- A member invite is refused on a vault with no organisation and when redeemed from a personal identity; a refused invite counts toward nothing.
- Offboarding behaves identically with a trial licence: revocation, wipe, the no-export rule, the audit and seat release have no trial exception.
- `docs/BUSINESS-TRIAL-ISSUING.md` states the issuer rules and the self-serve trial size (5 members, 10 computers, 14 days, one 14-day extension).
