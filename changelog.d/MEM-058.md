### Added

- Cloud AI apps that speak OAuth 2.1 connect without a URL that carries a secret: the server publishes its authorization server and protected resource metadata, the app registers itself, the owner approves a consent page on the vault's machine, and the app gets a bearer token it can refresh and revoke.
- The consent page is served and approved only on the vault's own machine: a remote authorize hands the browser a link to open there, the approve form carries a one-time value and is refused from any other origin, and a remote hit on the consent page is told where to open it.
- Open client registration is capped and expired consent codes are dropped on write, so the store does not grow without limit, and each registered app takes its own bus seat from its client name.

### Fixed

- Removing a device now revokes its OAuth tokens at once, and a connected app keeps its seat when the client cap is full.
- The authorize endpoint no longer redirects for an unknown client or an unregistered redirect URI, tokens are stored by hash instead of raw, a client only gets the scopes it registered with, and a non-loopback http redirect URI is refused at registration.
