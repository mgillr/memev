### Added

- `memev invite --teammate <name>` mints a one-time invite for a colleague, on the Team plan: their computer joins as `<name>-<machine>` and their apps' seats read `<app>@<name>-<machine>` on the bus roster. A teammate's computer meets the same checks as any invite first: a vault that cannot add a computer prints its plan line and no code, and without the secure link only `--local` mints a loopback address. Then, without the Team entitlement, the command prints the Team plan line and no code.
