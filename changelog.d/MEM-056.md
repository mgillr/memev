### Added

- Connector credentials are long-lived (a year) and revocable instead of expiring silently after 90 days, and a credential that will expire within 14 days now warns in `memev doctor`, `memev status` and the operator's resume card, with the renew and revoke commands.
- `memev token rotate` renews the credential's expiry (`--ttl`, default a year) and says the connector link must be pasted again, since `memev cloud url` prints the new one. A credential that is already past its expiry warns too, and a client that re-mints its credential revokes the record it replaces.
