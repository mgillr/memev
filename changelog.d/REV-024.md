### Added

- `memev compact` measures the vault from disk and reclaims space without deleting anything. `memev compact --dry-run` prints a size report (op-log bytes and segments, journal bytes and files, index bytes, git object bytes) and changes nothing.
- A real `memev compact` seals and archives the op-log segments into an archive folder and moves journal sessions older than the configured retention out of the index into `work/archive`; the archived log still verifies and materializes every note, and the retained sessions stay readable at their old path.
