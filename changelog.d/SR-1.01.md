### Fixed

- A daemon, `memev start`, `memev claims`, `memev doctor` and `memev compact` now live with the vault lock. The daemon exits with code 5 within 2 seconds when another process holds the vault (or takes the lock from it while it runs), leaves no `daemon.lock`, and says which pid holds it; `memev start` reports "the vault is locked by pid N" instead of "did not start", and names the port when the port is the problem. `memev claims` and `memev doctor` read the vault without taking the lock or writing anything; `memev doctor` names a held lock's pid, host and age, and reports a stale one as recoverable. `memev compact` is guarded by the lock, not by `daemon.json`. The systemd unit sets `RestartPreventExitStatus=5` and the launchd plist `ThrottleInterval` 60, so a refused daemon is not restarted every few seconds. `memev stop` no longer deletes the `daemon.lock` of a daemon that is still starting.
