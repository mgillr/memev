### Added

- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` shows what runs today, module by module, and one chart each for Personal, Business and Corporate. Business shows members' computers (Memev and local tools) syncing into one company vault that the admin console can see and search, one AI provider for the organisation (a cloud AI service or an on-premises LLM, over MCP), the paid crdt-merge convergence engine and relay, and a licence server that exchanges licence information only.
- `docs/MODULES.md` is the module interdependency map. `node scripts/docs/module-graph.mjs` writes it from the real imports in `bin/` and `src/` (a layer summary, a graph of every module and a per-module table), and `--check` and `test/docs-module-map.test.mjs` fail when it is out of date.
- `docs/CONVERGENCE.md` and a README section explain the paid core: the Memev core is Apache-2.0 and free, the convergence engine crdt-merge lives in its own repository under its original licence (currently BSL 1.1) and is called as an external dependency, never vendored, and only licence information passes between an install and our side.
- `docs/README.md` indexes every page and links back up; every page under `docs/` now links back to it. `docs/DEVELOPING.md` lists the checks to run, and `test/docs-links.test.mjs` fails on a broken relative link or anchor, a page that cannot be reached from the index, or a README that does not open with the centred video poster.

### Changed

- The README opens with the centred demo video poster (linked to `media/memev-tiers.mp4`), the title, badges and links. The 4 min 53 s cut with "Coming Soon" labels replaces the earlier 14 min 35 s video and GIF, which moved from `Demo Video/` to `media/`, outside the npm package.
- Pricing is now Personal with more computers at $12/month or $120/year (CHF 10 or CHF 100). Business and Corporate have no public price, and the Team and Enterprise columns are gone from `docs/PRICING.md`. The pricing tests hold the new figures and fail on a price from before 28 October 2026.
- Business and Corporate are described as one model: one company vault (Business) or one main corporate vault (Corporate) that all members' work data syncs into and the company can see and search, and one AI provider per organisation, either a cloud AI service or an on-premises LLM. Members keep private work outside Memev. A licence counts once per organisation, by person and by computer. The Business trial is 5 members and 10 computers, self-serve for one hosted cloud AI provider, with a sales-assisted pilot for on-premises, air-gapped and compliance-led organisations. All of it is marked planned. This replaces the work-vault-per-member wording.
- Personal is described as several cloud AI apps side by side (Claude, ChatGPT; more coming soon) plus local coding tools over MCP.
- `docs/LICENSING-NOTICE.md` names crdt-merge and its licence, and `CHARTER.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `docs/ISOLATION.md` and `docs/CONVERGENCE.md` say plainly what leaves a computer: a licence key on activation, the opt-in milestone report, and nothing from the vault.
