### Added

- The weekly funnel report adds a monthly verdict with `--month N`: each of the gates held in `scripts/metrics/gates.json` renders as a row with its threshold, its measured value, the month it falls due and pass, miss, not yet due or not measured, and a missed gate prints the branch text held for it in the file, word for word. A gate whose count file was not handed in reads not measured, never 0 and a miss. Paying users are the distinct paid licences issued; trials are not counted. The Business pilot gate is marked planned in the file, because no code records a completed pilot yet, and reads planned, not measured. A month that is not a whole number of months, 1 or more, is refused with an error instead of dropping the verdict.
