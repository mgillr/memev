### Changed
- `memev_recall` clamps `limit` to 1-10 whatever the caller sends, and `offset` pages through the remaining hits. The result says how many hits exist in total and, when there are more, that they can be fetched with an offset.
- Recall asks the index for the requested page, not for every match, so a large vault answers just as fast as a small one.
- The HTTP recall route leaves the paging hint off for CLI callers; the `next` field in the response carries it.
