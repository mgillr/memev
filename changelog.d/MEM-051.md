### Added

- With consent, Memev adds mcp__memev to the connected app's permissions.allow, so the app runs Memev's tools without a prompt each time. Consent is a question at memev init (on a terminal) or the --allow-tools flag on memev connect; memev disconnect removes the entry again. It is off unless you opt in, and it never touches your other permissions.

### Fixed

- A yes stored at init now survives a disconnect: the next memev connect puts mcp__memev back, and the checklist reads the entry from settings.json, so it never says the tools are allowed when the entry is gone.
