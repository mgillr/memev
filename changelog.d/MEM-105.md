### Added

- `memev import` reads project folders, AI history files, and chat export zips, previews what it found, and writes tagged, undoable notes into the vault.
- At init, the import step is offered once on a terminal after the lead question, off unless the user says yes. Init's own `--yes` never imports: an import needs a preview the person saw, or `memev import --yes`. Import makes no model calls and no network calls.
- `memev import` stops the daemon while it works on the vault and starts it again on every way out, a preview, a list, a refused undo or an answer of no included, with its usual settings.
