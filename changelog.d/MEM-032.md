### Added

- `memev bus hello <seat>` posts one hello to a seat, says what to do in the app, and waits for the seat to read it on its next turn, or exits 1 at the timeout.

### Fixed

- `memev bus hello operator` now resolves the alias to the lead's real seat before sending and waiting, so it reports read when the lead reads it; `memev bus hello all` is refused, because a hello is to one seat.
