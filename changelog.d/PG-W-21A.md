### Added

- A trust record, version 1 (`memev-trust/1`), with its validator and schema (`docs/schemas/trust-record-v1.json`), and a pure `evaluate` that answers whether a subject is eligible for a purpose (contribute or verify) in a scope as of a stated time. It denies by default, lets delegation only narrow (the same scope, the same or fewer purposes, no later expiry, no earlier start, a lower depth), and applies a revocation from its time and cascades it to every grant delegated from the revoked one. Every decision carries a rule id from one fixed, exported set. Roots are a parameter, there is no default root, and the module reads no clock, file, network or source of randomness. Trust is an eligibility input only: it never grants a permission, a membership or a read, and a personal scope is never shared. The record format is provisional (the two purposes, narrowing-only delegation and who may revoke are proposals in ADR 8, which is proposed), and the module is a library with tests that nothing calls yet.
