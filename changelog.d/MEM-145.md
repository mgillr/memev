### Added

- A person can bind a role to their own SSH key, verifiably and offline: the role's public key is signed with OpenSSH (namespace memev) and stored beside the role, and it verifies against an allowed_signers file.
- The setup step does the checks, the role key pair when absent, the bind and the receiver-side trust line in one rollback-safe step that leaves no partial artifact on failure, keeps a matching binding, and refuses to replace a binding to a different principal or key.
- Binding degrades cleanly when OpenSSH is missing or older than 8.0, with one clear message and the keys directory left untouched.
