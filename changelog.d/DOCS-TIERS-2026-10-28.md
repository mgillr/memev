### Changed

- The tier pages, the README and the related docs now describe four tiers: Personal on one computer (free), Personal with more computers (paid), and Business and Corporate (both marked coming). There is no Team tier: teammates join a company vault through an org admin's invite, on the business side only.
- Business and Corporate are described as one model at two scales: a work vault for each member plus a shared company vault, with promote and share up and company knowledge down. A personal or home vault never crosses into either, and anyone who leaves a company loses all vault access.
- The free tier is described as one computer at a time through your own GitHub. Git sync is single-writer backup, and a second computer that pushes gets a warning that concurrent edits can conflict. Editing on two machines at once is the paid tier and relies on a separately licensed merge engine that is not part of this repository.
- The tier pages for Business (`docs/tiers/business.md`) and Corporate (`docs/tiers/corporate.md`) replace the Business and Enterprise pages, and `docs/tiers/team.md` is removed. A Business trial is planned.
