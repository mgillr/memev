### Added

- `docs/scope-one/` is the takeover record a builder takes over with, taken from the baseline commit in `docs/scope-one/BASELINE.md`. It holds the requirement register (`REQUIREMENTS.md`, one row per requirement with a status), the traceability matrix (`TRACEABILITY.md`, each requirement traced to code, contract, dependency and test), the contract and work-item inventory (`INVENTORY.md`, every branch, contract and source with status and disposition), the build-order edges (`DEPENDENCIES.md`), the open ADRs and owner-only questions (`DECISIONS.md`), and the ordered slices with the deferred work (`SLICE-PLAN.md`).
- `test/scope-one-record.test.mjs` checks the record against itself: it fails on a missing or duplicate requirement, a status outside the set, a traceability row that cites a path, test or contract that is not real, an inventory row with a field or vocabulary outside the set, a dependency cycle or an edge naming an id the inventory does not hold, a missing ADR topic or owner question, a slice plan that names a contract the inventory does not hold, and a documentation index that does not list the record pages.
- `docs/README.md` indexes the seven record pages so each is reachable from the documentation index.
