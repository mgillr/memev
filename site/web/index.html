Operating infrastructure for human and AI work

Memev connects people, AI agents and tools through shared project state, coordination, governed execution, contracts and evidence-based verification. Its local vault preserves the knowledge and provenance that work depends on; the coordination and project layers help distinguish assigned, executed, verified and accepted work.

Explore the local Free Core or read the preview installation.

Personal

One person, one computer, free, with no licence and no account. The full core is included, and your notes back up to your own private GitHub repository. See Personal.

Business

One company vault that all members' work data syncs into, with an admin console that sees and searches it, and a provider policy for the whole organisation. Business and Corporate are planned and have no public price: contact us. See Business.

Trial

A Business trial is planned, with two paths: a self-serve trial for teams that use a hosted AI provider, and a sales-assisted pilot for organisations that run their own model. See the trial.
