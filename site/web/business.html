How it works

Every member's computer runs Memev and the member's local AI tools, which reach it over MCP. The work data of all members syncs into one company vault. The company can see and search everything in it through the company admin console. Members keep private work outside Memev.

The organisation connects to one AI provider: either one cloud AI service or a local model the organisation runs itself. The connection is over MCP. It is one provider for the whole organisation, not one per member.

Where it runs

The company server runs on the company's own server, in your office or your cloud account. Notes stay on your server and computers, and in your git backup. With a local model as the one provider, notes never leave your network. Only licence information passes between your install and ours, never vault data.
