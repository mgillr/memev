How it works

Every member's computer runs Memev and the member's local AI tools, which reach it over MCP. The work data of all members syncs into one company vault. The company can see and search everything in it through the company admin console. Members keep private work outside Memev.

Members use the AI providers and tools the organisation's policy allows, cloud AI services or a local model the organisation runs itself, over MCP. An organisation may restrict itself to one provider by policy, with egress rules and labels (planned).

Where it runs

There are two options, and the choice is recorded in the vault config.

Cloud-hosted planned

For teams that use an external hosted AI provider, or a mix of hosted and local models. You do not run the server; the operator does. The vault server runs on a host the operator manages, and your computers and AI apps connect to it. Calls to an AI provider are made by your own AI apps, with your own keys, and Memev holds no provider keys and makes no model calls. Your own git backup remains possible, and export always works. The service is planned and has no date.

On-prem

For teams with an internal model, strict data residency, or a compliance-led or air-gapped organisation. You run the server; nothing of yours runs on a host you do not control. The vault server runs on your own servers, and any internal model stays inside your environment. Memev holds no provider keys and makes no model calls. You run the vault server in a container with the image and compose file in this repository. In an air-gapped install the provider policy names a model inside your environment, and only licence information passes between your install and ours, never vault data.

Corporate is the same model at larger scale and with stricter control. There is one main corporate vault, and the organisation can be air-gapped.
