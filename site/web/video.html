Demo video

The demo film runs about five minutes and presents the product and its tier architecture. Some Business, Corporate and multi-computer scenes describe planned capabilities, not the current Free Core preview. Use the invited preview guide for the exact capabilities available to testers.

What the film shows

  • Title: the name of the product and the line that says what it is.
  • Personal: one person, one computer, the free core.
  • Personal with more computers: the same personal vault on several computers.
  • A hard boundary: personal and work never meet.
  • Business: a work vault for each member, plus the company vault.
  • Business functions: each function has its own scope.
  • Business reports: views over the company vault.
  • Business modules: CRM, project tools and ticketing.
  • Business trial: the cloud-hosted trial, with the full merge engine.
  • Business deployment options: where the company vault runs.
  • Optional secure remote setup: you start it, you can end it.
  • Corporate: the Business model at larger scale.
  • Corporate functions: who reads, owns and promotes what.
  • Provisioning and audit: members in, every step on record.
  • Corporate pilot: a guided start.
  • When someone leaves: access ends, keys are revoked, devices are removed.
  • The paid engine: merges changes made in several places at once.
  • How the tiers connect: personal and work stay separate, and the engine is called from outside the core.
  • End card.
