Overview video

The overview runs 4 minutes 38 seconds, with narration. It explains Memev as the infrastructure layer for connected AI and marks every part that is not built yet as planned. It shows no prices and no numbers for days, seats or computers.

What the film shows

  • Title: the name of the product and the line that says what it is.
  • The problem: every AI tool keeps its own memory, leaders see reports rather than the work, and no one can show what an AI was allowed to do.
  • The infrastructure layer: people, AI apps and agents, business applications and data systems connect to one layer with shared context, coordination, authority and evidence, and convergence (planned).
  • Keeping AI inside the rules: what connected AI may see, what it may do and what can be proved, and the limits of what Memev controls.
  • The executive view (planned): business inference intelligence, with questions asked of the organisation's own work record from the top down and every figure carrying its basis.
  • One layer at every scale: Personal in preview today; Business, Corporate and Federated planned.
  • The technology underneath: CRDT-Merge, E4 and ACFA-RS, each a separate project with a published reference, none part of the released Memev yet.
  • Where we are: a free development preview for one person; everything marked planned is not built yet.
