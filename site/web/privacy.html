Privacy

This site is a set of plain pages. It loads no script, sets no cookie and sends no data to any other host. The forms on this site go only to the address or endpoint we configure for them.

What the forms hold

The contact form and the trial request form ask for your organisation, your work e-mail, which path you are interested in, and your message. We use these details to answer you and to set up a trial where you ask for one. We do not share them with anyone else, and we delete them when the request is closed.

What Memev itself does

Memev runs on your own computer. Your notes stay in a plain Markdown vault that you own. There is no telemetry and nothing that reports anywhere unless you turn it on. Only licence information passes between an organisation install and ours, never vault data.
