What trust needs

Agreeing on one record is necessary. It is not enough.

1

Authority

Who may contribute, see or act.

2

Convergence

Everyone ends with the same record. This is CRDT-Merge.

3

Evidence

What was claimed, against what was checked.

4

Sovereignty

Each party keeps its own data, rules and operators.

Memev · Trust architecture for AI

Convergence makes every copy agree, but a converged record can still hold a mistaken or unauthorised contribution. Trust architecture is the four together: authority decides who may take part, convergence makes everyone agree, evidence separates a claim from a checked outcome, and sovereignty lets each site or partner keep control of its own data. Memev is the product layer that brings them together.
