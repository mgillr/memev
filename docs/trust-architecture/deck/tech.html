Memev · Trust architecture for AI

Three technologies sit under the layer, each with its own published reference. CRDT-Merge is the convergence engine: independent contributions resolve to the same result whatever order they arrive in, with no coordinator. Its paper is arXiv:2605.19373, Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types for Neural Network Model Merging: A Two-Layer Architecture Enabling CRDT-Compliant Model Merging Across 26 Strategies, May 2026. The paper addresses the merging of neural-network models; using the engine for organisational records is the planned extension. E4, short for Recursive Trust-Delta Entanglement, makes trust a dimension of the data itself, beside its causal history and its cryptographic hash, so trust travels with every record instead of sitting in a separate system. E4 has no arXiv paper; its reference is the master architecture specification in the crdt-merge repository. ACFA-RS gives results that every party can compute identically without a coordinator, and a contributor that sends conflicting versions leaves proof anyone can check. Its paper is arXiv:2607.10305, Byzantine Accountability Without Consensus, July 2026; it guarantees that honest parties agree on the result, not that the result is accurate. Together they are what would let independent organisations agree on one result, with trust carried in the record and outcomes anyone can recompute. E4 is part of CRDT-Merge. All three are separate from Memev and none is part of its released build yet.
