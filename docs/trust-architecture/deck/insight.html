Memev · Trust architecture for AI

Today a person can ask an AI about their own work. Nothing lets an executive team ask across the whole business, and what reaches the top is reported status, shaped at every level on the way up. The goal of Memev is business inference intelligence: because the layer holds one record of the work as it happens, linked to who did it, when and on what evidence, a leader can ask a question from the top down and get an answer drawn from the work itself. Each answer shows its sources and when they were last confirmed, and keeps what is verified apart from what an AI inferred. In Memev's design an inference stays a hypothesis until an authorised person or an approved check confirms it, and an AI cannot confirm its own finding. This capability is planned; the finding rule is built as a data model and is not yet used by the product.
