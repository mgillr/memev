Memev · Trust architecture for AI

The same layer is worth something different at each scale. A single user stops re-explaining a project to every AI tool, and keeps the record on their own computer; this runs today as a preview. A small business gets one company record that its people and AI tools share under company rules, so knowledge stays when staff or AI vendors change. A global business coordinates programmes across sites in different countries while each site keeps its own authority and its data stays in its jurisdiction. A federation lets separate businesses, suppliers and vendors work on shared programmes, sharing only what each agreement allows, without any of them handing over systems or data. The last three are planned.
