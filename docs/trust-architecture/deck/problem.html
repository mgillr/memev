Memev · Trust architecture for AI

AI is spreading across personal tools, teams, departments and business systems, but it is siloed: each person can use AI on their own work, and none of it connects. So the executive team cannot ask across the business. What reaches the top is reported status, shaped at every level on the way up, and it is hard to establish what was agreed, what was done, and what was actually checked.
