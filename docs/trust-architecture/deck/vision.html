The vision

One infrastructure layer for connected intelligence, at every scale.

Personal

One person

Every AI tool they use works from one shared context.

Business

One company

Teams, agents and company systems work under one set of rules.

Corporate

Many sites

Offices in many countries. Each site keeps its own authority.

Federated

Many firms

Partners, suppliers and customers share only what they agree to.

The layer stays the same. What grows is the trust boundary it has to cross.

Memev · Trust architecture for AI

The same product direction extends from one person, through a business and a multi-site corporation, to independent organisations working together. Business and Corporate sit inside one organisation. Federated is the distinct step: separate owners, separate rules, shared work.
