Memev · Trust architecture for AI

Memev is infrastructure, not another AI application. It sits beneath whichever AI an organisation uses and above the people, projects and systems that do the work. The layer supplies five things: a shared context, coordination between people and AI, authority over who may see and act, evidence of what was done and checked, and convergence to one agreed result, which is the part CRDT-Merge is planned to provide. Because it is a layer and not an application, the AI on top can change without losing the organisation's connected record.
