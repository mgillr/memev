# Team: 2 to 20 people

> **DRAFT.** A key for each person (member keys) is planned; until it ships, Team is contact us.
> The shared vault over the secure link, a key for each computer and provenance work today; see the
> [status table](README.md#what-is-built-and-what-is-planned).

For a small team that wants one memory for its people and their AI apps, without running a company
hub.

## What's included

- Everything in Free on every computer
- One shared vault that every computer reaches over the secure link (built)
- A key for each computer, removed with one command (built); a key for each person (planned)
- Provenance: who wrote each memory and which session used it (built)
- The comms bus across the team's AI apps: seats, roster, comms checks, tasks and holds (built)
- Backup to your own private GitHub repository (built)

## How it is counted

- Sold per person, at least 2. Each person brings 3 computers and 10 cloud AI connectors, pooled
  into one licence, so a team of 5 can enrol 15 computers in any mix.
- The licence carries the computer count and nothing else, and it is checked offline. Past the
  count, the vault refuses the next computer; `memev devices remove <id>` frees a place at once.

They overlap at 10 to 20 people on purpose. Choose [Business](sme.md) for the company hub, sign-in
with Google or Microsoft, departments and roles, restricted notes and the egress log. Choose Team
when one shared vault is enough.
